## 📊 Model Performance Summary

### Classification Results (Sleep Quality Prediction)

| Model | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1 | ROC-AUC |
|-------|----------|-----------|--------|-----|---------|
| Deep NN | **87.6%** | 87.3% | 85.7% | 86.5% | **0.959** |
| Perceptron | 87.5% | **94.4%** | 77.7% | 85.2% | 0.958 |
| Logistic Reg | 87.3% | 84.1% | **89.5%** | **86.7%** | 0.958 |
| SVM (RBF) | 87.5% | 87.0% | 85.0% | 86.0% | 0.958 |

**Key Insight**: All models achieve >87% accuracy, demonstrating robust feature engineering and preprocessing.

### Statistical Findings

1. **Stress-Sleep Correlation**: Significant negative correlation (p < 0.05)
2. **Activity Impact**: Intense activity correlates with better sleep
3. **Heart Rate Patterns**: Strong predictor of activity classification
